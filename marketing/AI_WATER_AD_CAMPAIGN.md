# Chill Pros Water — AI-Assisted Advertising Campaign

**Market:** San Antonio / Bexar County  
**Primary goal:** Generate qualified residential water-treatment assessments and convert them into installations and recurring WaterCare customers.

## Campaign positioning

### Primary message
**San Antonio has hard water. Chill Pros can test it, explain it, and build the right treatment solution for your home.**

Avoid fear-based messaging. Do not imply that compliant municipal water is unsafe.

### Offer
**Launch: Complimentary Chill Pros Water Assessment**

CTA options:
- Book My Water Assessment
- Test My Water
- Get My Water Report
- Call Chill Pros Water

## Campaign structure

### Campaign A — High-intent search
Target intent around:
- water softener San Antonio
- water softener installation San Antonio
- water filtration San Antonio
- whole house water filtration
- reverse osmosis installation San Antonio
- water softener repair San Antonio
- water softener service San Antonio

Use separate ad groups for installation, filtration/RO, and repair/service.

### Campaign B — Local social discovery
Creative themes:
1. San Antonio hard-water education
2. Before/after scale education without exaggerated claims
3. Technician water testing
4. Home water-treatment equipment
5. Transparent three-option package comparison
6. Repair/service for existing systems

### Campaign C — Retargeting
Audience:
- Landing-page visitors
- Video viewers
- Lead-form openers who did not submit
- Existing Chill Pros customers where legally and platform-policy appropriate

### Campaign D — Existing-customer cross-sell
Use existing Chill Pros customer relationships to introduce residential water assessment. Do not assume commercial contacts are homeowners; use explicit opt-in/appropriate contact rules.

## Initial budget test

Suggested launch test:

## AI creative engine

For each campaign, generate multiple variants across:
- Hook
- Headline
- Primary text
- CTA
- Offer framing
- Local proof
- Educational angle
- Service angle

AI should produce variants, but a human-approved source-of-truth library controls factual claims.

### Approved factual anchors
- San Antonio/SAWS water hardness is typically about 15–20 grains per gallon.
- Chill Pros Water is a residential water-treatment division of Chill Pros.
- Services include softening, whole-home filtration, RO, installation and service as legally permitted.
- Pricing is quote-based and depends on equipment and site conditions.

### Prohibited AI claims
- “Your tap water is dangerous” without substantiation.
- “Removes all contaminants.”
- Unsupported health benefits.
- Fake review/testimonial language.
- Fake scarcity.
- Fake discounts.
- Unverified certifications.
- Guaranteed savings or guaranteed water-quality outcomes.

## AI lead-response workflow

When a lead arrives, AI drafts a response using approved templates and the lead's information.

Lead fields:
- Name
- Phone
- ZIP code
- Home type
- Household size
- Existing softener/filtration system
- Primary concern
- Preferred appointment window
- Lead source

Qualification logic:
- San Antonio/Bexar service area?
- Residential property?
- New installation, filtration/RO, or service?
- Existing softener?
- Existing softener loop?
- Urgency?
- Appointment requested?

AI should never promise a price, permit outcome, installation date, water-quality result, or licensing status unless that information is already confirmed by the operating system.

## Follow-up sequence

### Immediately
Lead acknowledgement + booking link/phone CTA.

### 2 hours
Short educational message about San Antonio hard water + reminder to book assessment.

### 24 hours
Explain the assessment and what the homeowner receives.

### 72 hours
Service/installation option reminder.

### 7 days
Final launch-offer reminder, without false scarcity.

- Assessment show rate
- Quote rate
- Close rate

Then propose budget/creative changes for human approval.
