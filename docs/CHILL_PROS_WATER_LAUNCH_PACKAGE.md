# Chill Pros Water — San Antonio Launch Package

**Status:** Launch package initiated  
**Division:** Chill Pros Water  
**Market:** San Antonio / Bexar County  
**Launch objective:** Establish residential water treatment as a service-first Chill Pros division.

## 1. Initial offer architecture

### Essential — $2,495–$3,495 installed
- Whole-home water softener
- Sizing based on household demand and measured hardness
- Standard installation where plumbing conditions permit
- Commissioning and customer orientation

### Complete — $3,495–$4,995 installed
- Whole-home water softener
- Sediment/carbon filtration configuration as appropriate
- Commissioning
- Customer water report

### Pure — $4,495–$6,495 installed
- Whole-home softener
- Whole-home filtration
- Reverse-osmosis drinking-water system
- Commissioning and customer water report

### RO — $1,195–$1,995 installed
- Dedicated drinking-water reverse-osmosis system
- Installation and commissioning

> Pricing is launch-target pricing, not a final price book. Final prices must be validated against equipment cost, labor, permits, site conditions, warranty exposure, and licensing requirements.

## 4. Customer acquisition offer

### Chill Pros Water Assessment

Use a low-friction assessment as the primary lead-generation offer rather than selling a system immediately.

Assessment workflow:
1. Capture homeowner and property information.
2. Record existing equipment and installation location.
3. Test hardness and other appropriate water characteristics.
4. Evaluate household size and water demand.
5. Inspect plumbing/treatment configuration.
6. Produce a plain-language water report.
7. Recommend only the treatment components supported by the findings.
8. Present Essential / Complete / Pure options when applicable.

Initial campaign offer can be promoted as a complimentary launch assessment; a paid assessment option can be introduced later.

## 6. Licensing and compliance gate

Before paid installation work begins, verify the exact TCEQ Water Treatment Specialist requirements and City of San Antonio registration/permit requirements for the planned scope. Do not assume existing HVAC/refrigeration credentials authorize potable-water treatment installation.

Launch gate:
- Confirm license pathway
- Confirm company registration requirements
- Confirm permit requirements
- Confirm who may perform each plumbing connection
- Confirm inspection requirements
- Confirm insurance coverage
- Confirm equipment certification/warranty requirements

## 7. Equipment strategy

Do not launch with a large catalog.

Standardize around:
- One primary softener platform
- One secondary/value platform
- One RO platform
- One sediment/carbon filtration family
- Standard fittings, bypasses, tubing, valves and service parts

Selection criteria:
- Serviceability
- Parts availability
- Warranty
- Flow capacity
- San Antonio hardness suitability
- Installer documentation
- OEM support
- Margin
